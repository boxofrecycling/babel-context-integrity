## Reproducibility

The **wheel is bit-reproducible**: rebuilding from a fresh clone of the
same commit, on the same Python, produces a byte-identical file. The
digest above was confirmed identical across two independent trees.

The **sdist is content-reproducible but not bit-reproducible**: its
extracted tree is identical across builds, while the gzip container
embeds a timestamp, so the outer digest changes. That is a property of
the archive format, not of the contents, and it is recorded here rather
than papered over.

Verify either claim yourself:

```bash
shasum -a 256 dist/*
# wheel: rebuild and compare digests
# sdist: extract both and `diff -r` the trees
```

`release/DIGESTS.txt` is deliberately excluded from the sdist: an
archive cannot contain its own hash.
